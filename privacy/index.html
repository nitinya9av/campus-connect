Privacy Policy

Application: Campus Connect

01 · Commitment to User Privacy

Campus Connect ("the Application") is an open-source utility designed exclusively for students, faculty, and staff of the Central University of Rajasthan (CURAJ) to automate login against the campus Wi-Fi captive portal network.

We hold an absolute, uncompromising commitment to user privacy: We do not collect, harvest, store on remote servers, monetize, or share any personal data or usage analytics. The Application executes 100% client-side on your device.

02 · Data Handled & Storage

To perform automated captive portal login on your behalf, the Application handles the following information:

  • Username (Mobile Number): Used solely to construct the standard authentication payload required by the university gateway.
  • Wi-Fi Password: Used solely to authenticate with the official CURAJ campus gateway.

Local Storage Only: Your credentials are saved exclusively within the secure, private local storage of your device (Android AsyncStorage / SharedPreferences and Windows %APPDATA%). Credentials are never sent to, proxied through, or backed up to any developer-controlled servers or third parties.

03 · Network Transmission & Gateway Destination

When authenticating, your credentials are submitted directly over your local campus Wi-Fi network to the official university network access hardware:

  • Official CURAJ Gateway: http://122.252.242.93/userportal/newlogin.do
  • Inventum NAS Controller: http://1.254.254.254/ (used for firewall route authorization)
  • Connectivity Probes: https://www.google.com/generate_204 (standard zero-content probe used solely to verify active internet throughput)

At no point are credentials transmitted to any third-party domain, advertising network, or cloud service.

04 · Android Device Permissions

The Android application requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to function:

  • INTERNET — Required to send HTTP authentication requests to the campus gateway and verify internet access.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE & ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — Required to detect when the device connects to the campus Wi-Fi network so auto-login can trigger.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC — Required on Android to maintain a background network listener that automatically re-authenticates the device when Wi-Fi reconnects or sessions expire, without requiring the user to manually open the app.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required by modern Android to run the background connection service with a minimized, silent status indication.

05 · Data Deletion & Deregistration

You retain complete control over your stored credentials at all times:

  • In-App Deregistration: Tapping the "Deregister" button inside the app immediately and permanently erases all saved credentials from your device's local storage and stops background services.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Campus Connect automatically wipes all application data, local storage, and cached files managed by your operating system.

06 · Third-Party Services & SDKs

Campus Connect does not integrate any third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics), advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob), user tracking tools, or remote crash reporting frameworks.

07 · Open Source & Transparency

Campus Connect is free, open-source software licensed under the MIT License. The complete source code for both the Windows desktop client and Android mobile app is public, auditable, and open for community review on GitHub:

https://github.com/nitinya9av/campus-connect
