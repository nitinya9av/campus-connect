Campus Connect

CURAJ WI-FI · 1-CLICK AUTOMATION

Automates the captive portal login for Central University of Rajasthan. Client-side, zero admin required, and reversible in one click. Available for Windows & Android.

Windows · 1-Click Portable App

Native, lightweight auto-login client for Windows.

A single, standalone portable application. Download, enter your credentials once, and click Register. It runs silently in the background whenever connected to CURAJ Wi-Fi.

Download Windows Package (.msix) Portable (.exe)
v1.0 · Modern Windows Package (.msix) · Portable .exe (25.5 KB)
Free code signing provided by the SignPath Foundation.

Setup Guide

  • 01 Install campus-connect.msix (or launch the portable campus-connect.exe).
  • 02 Enter your Mobile Number (Username) and Password, then click Register Auto-Login.
  • 03 Done! Your PC will now keep you authenticated automatically on startup. To turn it off anytime, open the app and click Deregister.
100% Portable & Safe: No installer or admin elevation required. Stores your credentials strictly on your machine and communicates directly with the campus gateway.

Android · Google Play Store

Zero-click background Wi-Fi auto-login for your Android phone.

Install directly from the Google Play Store. Enter your credentials once, and Campus Connect keeps you authenticated automatically in the background whenever you connect to CURAJ Wi-Fi across hostels, academic departments, or the library.

Get it on Google Play

No Location No Contacts No Files / Photos Zero Tracking
Zero Sensitive Permissions · 100% Safe for Students

Campus Connect requests zero sensitive device permissions. It cannot access your location, photos, files, contacts, camera, or microphone. It only uses standard local network communication to send captive portal login requests directly to the university gateway (122.252.242.93).

Setup Guide

  • 01 Install Campus Connect from the Google Play Store.
  • 02 Enter your Mobile Number and Password, then tap Login & Register.
  • 03 Done! Your phone will now reconnect and authenticate automatically whenever connected to CURAJ Wi-Fi. To stop, tap Deregister anytime.
Zero Sensitive Permissions & 100% Private: Credentials remain encrypted solely in your phone's private storage and are communicated only to the local CURAJ gateway.

Security · Transparency

  • Zero Data Collection: Executed 100% client-side. Passwords are never sent to or logged on any external server.
  • Direct Gateway: HTTP requests target the official university captive portal (122.252.242.93).
  • Zero Admin Elevation: Runs in standard user space without modifying system-level OS files.
  • 100% Open Source: Complete C# source code and project files are public and auditable on GitHub.
