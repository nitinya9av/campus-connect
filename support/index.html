Support Campus Connect

COMMUNITY · DEVELOPER SUSTAINABILITY · OPEN ECOSYSTEM

Campus Connect is 100% free and open-source software built for every student, researcher, and faculty member at the Central University of Rajasthan.

Our mission is simple: eliminate captive portal friction completely. We believe campus internet should connect instantly without manual logins or complex hurdles. To distribute trusted, signed applications on official app stores and eliminate security warnings for students, we face direct developer registration fees. Your support helps us recover past expenses and expand official distribution across all major platforms.

Platform Distribution Goals

$25 · Funded & Active

Google Play Store (Android)

A $25 one-time developer registration fee was paid out-of-pocket to publish the official Android application on the Google Play Store with silent 24/7 background reconnection. Help us recover this initial personal investment so we can continue maintaining the app for all university students.

$19 · Target Goal #1

Microsoft Store (Windows 10 & 11)

A $19 developer fee allows us to publish our modern packaged application (campus-connect.msix) directly to the official Microsoft Store. This will provide 1-click installation directly from Windows, automatic background updates, and complete elimination of Windows SmartScreen warning screens.

$99/year · Target Goal #2

Apple App Store (iOS & iPadOS)

Apple strictly requires an active $99/year Apple Developer Program membership to compile, sign, and publish native applications to the iOS App Store. This will enable us to ship a native iOS/iPadOS app that solves background Wi-Fi authentication without needing manual Shortcuts or complex workarounds.
