iOS & iPadOS Automation Guide

NATIVE APPLE SHORTCUTS · AUTOMATED WI-FI LOGIN · IPHONE & IPAD

Zero App Store Install Native Wi-Fi Event Trigger iCloud Auto-Sync (iPhone + iPad) 0% Standby Battery Usage

Because iOS aggressively freezes background apps, third-party apps cannot reliably maintain background network listeners. Instead, Campus Connect leverages Apple's built-in Shortcuts Personal Automation engine. Whenever your iPhone or iPad detects the campus Wi-Fi network, iOS automatically wakes up and triggers the login request silently in the background.

Step 01

Create a New Personal Automation

Every iPhone and iPad comes with Apple's official Shortcuts app pre-installed (iOS 13+):

  • 1 Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone or iPad.
  • 2 Tap the Automation tab located in the bottom navigation bar.
  • 3 Tap the + (Plus) icon in the top-right corner to create a new automation.
Step 02

Set the Wi-Fi Trigger

Configure iOS to trigger the shortcut whenever your device connects to the campus network:

  • 1 Scroll down and tap Wi-Fi.
  • 2 Tap Choose next to Network, and select CURAJ-WIFI (or your hostel / department SSID).
  • 3 Under When, ensure Is Connected is checked.
Crucial Setting for Silent Background Reconnection:

Select "Run Immediately" and make sure "Notify When Run" is turned OFF. This ensures your iPhone connects silently without asking for permission or showing banners every time!

Step 03

Add the 3 Fast-Path Authentication Actions

Tap New Blank Automation, then tap Add Action. We will add 3 quick network actions:

Action 1 · Prime Gateway Session

Search for "Get Contents of URL" and set the URL to:

http://1.254.254.254/

Action 2 · Send Login Authentication (POST)

Search for "Get Contents of URL" again and set the URL to:

http://122.252.242.93/userportal/newlogin.do

Tap the dropdown arrow (Show More) on this action and configure:

  • Method: Select POST
  • Request Body: Select Form

Tap Add new field → Text for each of these 5 parameters:

Key Value Notes
username Your Mobile Number e.g. 9876543210
password Your Wi-Fi Password Your university portal password
phone 0 Constant value 0
type 2 Constant value 2
jsonresponse 1 Constant value 1

Action 3 · Activate Firewall Bypass

Add one final "Get Contents of URL" action:

http://1.254.254.254/

(Triggers the Inventum NAS route opening to activate outward WAN traffic).

Tap Done in the top-right corner to save!
Step 04

Test & Verify

  1. 1 Swipe down to open your iPhone's Control Center.
  2. 2 Tap the Wi-Fi icon to turn it off, then tap it again to reconnect to CURAJ-WIFI.
  3. 3 Within 1 to 2 seconds, the automation executes silently in the background and full internet access is active!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this shortcut sync with my iPad?

Yes! If you have iCloud enabled for Shortcuts, the actions will sync automatically. You can enable the Wi-Fi automation trigger on both your iPhone and iPad so both devices stay logged in.

What if iOS prompts me to confirm before running?

Open the Shortcuts app → Automation tab → tap your Wi-Fi automation. Make sure that Run Immediately is selected and Notify When Run is toggled OFF.

How do I update my password or stop the automation?

To update your password, open the automation in Shortcuts, tap into Action 2, and edit the password field. To disable it, simply swipe left on the automation in Shortcuts and tap Delete.
